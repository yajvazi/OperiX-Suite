# OperiX Kosovo commercial-document matrix

Status: implementation baseline and legal-control register  
Reviewed: 2026-08-12  
Scope: OperiX Invoice Mobile first, with the shared Supabase/domain contract used by Web.

This matrix separates the document's legal/commercial meaning from the economic events that may follow it. “No” means that creating/issuing that document alone must not cause the effect. “Conditional” means that a separate verified event, tax point, accounting policy, return, or configured fiscalization obligation is required. A proforma is intentionally not treated as an invoice: under Kosovo VAT Law Article 22(3.2), an actual invoice issued before supply can itself trigger VAT, so OperiX must keep a preliminary proforma outside the tax-invoice path.

## Important legal distinctions used by the implementation

- A summary invoice under Article 44(2) and an electronic invoice under Article 50 are modes/issuance features, not unrelated commercial-document tables.
- Article 45 is the B2B field contract; Article 46 is the other-person/simplified field contract. The mobile builder therefore keeps customer classification and tax-field validation separate from document presentation.
- Article 22 determines the chargeable event. The app must not substitute `issue_date` for `supply_date`, `payment_date`, or the received-advance date without the tax engine deciding that the dates are equivalent.
- Article 47 requires the correction document to identify the original invoice, the reason, and corrected taxable/VAT amounts. The original issued invoice is never edited away.
- Article 49 and Administrative Instruction MF 01/2026 keep ordinary commercial documents separate from SEF/EFS fiscalization. EFS eligibility is a separate status and provider boundary, not a document-title flag.

This is an engineering control register, not a legal opinion. The matrix deliberately records conditional behavior where the law or the accounting policy requires a fact that the software cannot safely infer. Before production activation, a Kosovo tax/accounting professional must approve the configured VAT tax-point rules, advance ledger mapping, bad-debt workflow, and the organization's EFS eligibility/certification.
