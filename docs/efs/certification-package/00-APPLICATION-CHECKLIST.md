# Application checklist

Official source: [TAK EFS Conditions and Procedures, final 29.05.2026](https://www.atk-ks.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ENG-KUSHTET-DHE-PROCEDURAT-PER-APLIKIMIN-CERTIFIKIMIN-dhe-MIREMBAJTJEN-e-SEF-Verzioni-Final-29.05.2026.pdf), Article 5.

| Official item | Provider | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Signed standard EFS application form | Applicant | REQUIRED FROM APPLICANT |
| Applicant identity copy where applicable | Applicant | REQUIRED FROM APPLICANT |
| KBRA business registration certificate | Applicant | REQUIRED FROM APPLICANT |
| Consortium TAK fiscal certificate where applicable | Applicant/TAK | REQUIRED FROM APPLICANT |
| General software description, integration, and technologies | OperiX/applicant | Draft in this package |
| User manual | OperiX/applicant | Draft in this package |
| Signed compliance statement | Applicant | REQUIRED FROM APPLICANT |
| Additional evidence requested by TAK | TAK/applicant | Pending |

TAK testing and the final certificate/identification number are outside software-only authority. Missing documents may be requested and corrected under the official procedure.
