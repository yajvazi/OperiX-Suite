# Suppliers, supplier bills, and bill scanning

## Create a vendor

1. Open **More > Purchases** or the vendor list from the Business area.
2. Tap add.
3. Enter vendor name and optional email, phone, address, city, ZIP, country, tax ID, and notes.
4. Save.

The current vendor form includes a Check Registry link and stores the vendor under the active company.

## Create a supplier bill

1. Open the supplier-bill list.
2. Tap add.
3. Select a vendor.
4. Enter bill number, issue date, due date, status, tax amount, notes, and line items.
5. For each line enter description, quantity, unit price, and review the calculated amount.
6. Save.

The total is subtotal plus the entered tax amount. The form can edit an existing bill by replacing its line rows.

## Scan a bill

Use **Scan bill** when available. Recognized vendor name, bill number, date, and lines are passed into the form. Review every value before saving; scanning is a prefill helper and not an automatic accounting approval.
