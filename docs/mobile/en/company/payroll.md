# Payroll screen

Payroll is opened from **More** for users who are not restricted as workers and from the Tax Center where the shortcut is available.

The current mobile screen reads payroll runs, payslip snapshots, and payroll liabilities for the accessible company scope. It presents payroll status/summary information and links to the records returned by the backend.

The audited source does not establish a complete mobile payroll-run creation, employee self-service, payslip distribution, or tax-filing workflow. Treat the screen as a read/management entry point governed by backend permissions, not as proof that all payroll operations are available from mobile.
