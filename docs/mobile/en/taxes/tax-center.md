# Tax Center and tax books

Open **More > Taxes**. The screen reads the EFS status for the selected company scope and displays links to tax/accounting views.

## Available links

- Sales Book — posted sales and output VAT view;
- Purchase Book — supplier invoices and input VAT view;
- Cash & Payments — cash/bank journal view;
- Payroll — payroll PIT/pension entry point, subject to role;
- Withholding Tax — recorded withholding transactions;
- Declarations — declaration preview/status;
- Archived Documents — source-document archive view;
- Tax Calendar — tax obligations/deadlines view.

Each link opens a report preview backed by a server-side source. See [Reports](../reports/financial-reports.md) for row limits and export behavior.

## VAT and tax dates

The mobile invoice form stores issue, due, supply/order/delivery, and payment-related date fields where applicable. It does not replace the centralized backend tax logic or provide a legal interpretation of a tax point. Review the current company accounting/tax procedure before issuing or correcting a document.

## EFS warning

The current screen displays `EFS NOT CERTIFIED` when no accepted status is found and states that production EFS is disabled. See [Fiscalization status](../fiscalization/status-and-eligibility.md).
